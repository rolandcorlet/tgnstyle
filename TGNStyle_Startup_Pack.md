## 1. One-Page Launch & Compliance Checklist (Mount Annan, NSW)

> Complete steps 1–3 before ordering printed signage or paying for website hosting so your ABN and business name are officially locked in.

### Phase A: Government & Brand Registration
- [ ] **1. Apply for an ABN (Free)** via the [Australian Government Business Registration Service (BRS)](https://register.business.gov.au/).
- [ ] **2. Register Business Name ("TGN Style")** via BRS/ASIC ($47 for 1 year or $108 for 3 years).
- [ ] **3. Register Domain Names** via an auDA-accredited registrar (e.g., VentraIP):
  - [ ] `tgnstyle.com.au` (Primary website)
  - [ ] `tgnstyles.com.au` (Redirect to primary)
  - [ ] `tgnstyle.au` (Short link for Instagram bio)
- [ ] **4. Secure Social Media Handles:** Claim `@tgnstyle` / `@tgn.style` on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook, plus your **Google Business Profile** for *"TGNStyle Mount Annan"*.
- [ ] **5. Open a Dedicated Business Bank Account** under "TGN Style" to keep salon income and expenses separate for tax time.

### Phase B: NSW & Camden Council Compliance
- [ ] **6. Qualification File (*Hairdressers Act 2003*):** Keep a copy of Teagan's **Certificate III in Hairdressing** (and any staff qualifications) on-site for NSW Fair Trading compliance.
- [ ] **7. Premises Approval (Camden Council):**
  - *If Home Salon:* Obtain Complying Development Certificate (CDC) or Development Application (DA) approval for a Home Business from Camden Council.
  - *If Commercial Shopfront:* Verify zoning/use approval for hair and beauty premises.
- [ ] **8. Fit-out Hygiene Check (NSW Health Standards):**
  - [ ] Dedicated handwashing basin with warm running water, liquid soap, and single-use paper towels.
  - [ ] Separate sink for cleaning tint bowls, brushes, and equipment.
  - [ ] Smooth, wipeable, impervious flooring and work surfaces.
  - [ ] Hospital-grade disinfectant & barbicide/UV cabinet for combs, scissors, and tools.
- [ ] **9. Council Health Notification:** Submit the **"Notification Form – Beauty, Hairdresser, Barber, Skin Penetration Procedures"** to Camden Council prior to opening day.
- [ ] **10. Safety Data Sheets (SDS) Folder:** Create a physical or digital binder containing SDS PDFs for all hair colours, bleaches, peroxide developers, toners, lash/brow tints, waxes, and disinfectants used in the salon.

### Phase C: Insurance & Systems
- [ ] **11. Salon Insurance:** Secure **Public Liability** AND **Professional Indemnity / Treatment Risk Insurance** (verify it explicitly covers chemical colouring/bleaching and beauty treatments).
- [ ] **12. Booking & POS System:** Set up **Timely**, **Fresha**, or **Square Appointments** connected to Stripe/Square for automated booking deposits and SMS reminders.

## 2. Salon Booking, Deposit & Cancellation Policy
*Copy and paste this onto your `tgnstyle.com.au` booking page and turn the short version into an Instagram Story Highlight.*

### Website / Booking Software Version

#### **TGNStyle — Booking & Cancellation Policy**
At **TGNStyle**, we dedicate exclusive time and bespoke preparation to every client. To ensure fairness to both our stylists and clients on our waitlist, all appointments are subject to the following policy:

1. **Booking Deposits**
   * A **25% non-refundable booking deposit** (or **$50 minimum** for colour/balayage packages) is required at the time of booking to secure your appointment.
   * Your deposit is deducted straight from the final total of your service on the day of your appointment.

2. **Rescheduling & Cancellations (48 Hours' Notice)**
   * We kindly require at least **48 hours' notice** if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment.
   * With 48+ hours' notice, your deposit will be seamlessly transferred to your new appointment date.
   * Cancellations or reschedules made with **less than 48 hours' notice** will forfeit the booking deposit, and a new deposit will be required to rebook.

3. **No-Shows & Late Arrivals**
   * **No-Shows:** Failure to attend an appointment without notice will result in forfeiture of your deposit and may require 50% prepayment for future bookings.
   * **Late Arrivals:** Please let us know if you are running behind. Arrivals more than **15 minutes late** may require us to adjust or shorten your service (e.g., skipping a blow-wave or simplifying colour work) so we do not delay the next client.

4. **Service Adjustments on the Day**
   * If you book a major colour package (e.g., Full Head Foils / Balayage) and decide at your appointment to change to a shorter service (e.g., Trim or Root Tint only) without 48 hours' notice, a fee may still apply for the reserved chair time.

5. **Satisfaction Guarantee & Redo Policy**
   * We want you to love your hair! If you require a toner tweak or minor adjustment, please contact us within **7 days** of your original appointment. Complimentary adjustments do not apply if there is a change of mind regarding the agreed consultation or if non-salon-approved home care products have been used.
