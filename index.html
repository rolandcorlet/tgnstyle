Studio Etiquette & FAQ

Booking Policies & Common Questions

Clear, transparent guidelines so every client enjoys an unhurried, luxury studio experience.

Policy 01

Booking Deposits

A 25% deposit ($50 minimum on colour & balayage packages) secures your appointment and comes straight off your final bill on the day.

Policy 02

48 Hours' Notice

Need to move your booking? Provide 48+ hours' notice and your deposit rolls over seamlessly. Less than 48 hours' notice forfeits the deposit.

Policy 03

Late Arrivals

Please message us if you are running late. Arrivals 15+ minutes late may require simplifying your service so we do not delay the next client.

Policy 04

7-Day Care Promise

We want you to love your hair! If you need a minor toner tweak, reach out within 7 days of your visit and we will happily look after you.
