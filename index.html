Studio Etiquette

Booking, Deposit & Cancellation Policy

At TGNStyle, we dedicate exclusive chair time and bespoke preparation to every client.

1. Booking Deposits

A 25% booking deposit ($50 minimum for colour & balayage packages) secures your appointment and is deducted directly from your final bill on the day.

2. 48 Hours' Notice

Need to reschedule? Provide at least 48 hours' notice and your deposit transfers seamlessly to your new date. Less than 48 hours' notice forfeits the deposit.

3. Punctuality

Please message us if you are running behind. Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may require adjusting your service so we do not delay the next client.

4. 7-Day Care Promise

We want you to adore your hair! If you need a minor toner tweak, reach out within 7 days of your visit and we will happily look after you.
