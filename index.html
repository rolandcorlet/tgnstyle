Before you book

Questions we get asked in the chair (so you don't have to wonder)

Every melt is finished with our gloss-seal treatment and you leave with a custom after-care plan (wash frequency, the one product you actually need). Most clients see 6–8 weeks of true colour before a gentle refresh — not the "brassy by Friday" you've had elsewhere.
That's exactly what the free consult is for. We map tone, placement and intensity against your skin tone and lifestyle before a single foil goes in, and we always offer a "testable" version first — a toned-down placement you can size up from later.
A full colour melt with cut and gloss typically runs 3–4 hours. A precision cut alone is closer to 60–75 minutes. We'll give you an exact window when you book so you're not stuck guessing.
Constantly. Your consult includes a strand test and, if needed, a bond-repair add-on before we colour. If your hair isn't ready for vivid yet, we'll tell you honestly and build a plan to get it there — we'd rather wait than wreck it.
Pricing depends on hair length, density and how much lift the colour needs — your free consult ends with an exact, no-surprises quote before anything is booked in.
